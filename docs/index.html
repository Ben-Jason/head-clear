This app tracks the plan your clinician gave you; it doesn't replace them or give medical advice, and the pass/fail flags are only a convenience. Your data and your plan file stay on this device. The only things sent anywhere are your reminder subscription and your next BCTT date, which live in your own Cloudflare Worker so the right reminder can fire.
