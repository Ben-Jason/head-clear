Why an assessment, why now

Three return-to-play attempts have each brought symptoms back, and you're past the four-week mark since the original injury. Under the Amsterdam 2023 International Consensus Statement on Concussion in Sport, that combination — symptoms persisting beyond four weeks plus repeated failed attempts — is specifically when you're meant to be referred to a clinician with concussion expertise, not just told to rest longer. A doctor, sports physician or concussion-trained physio can check for things self-managed rest can't fix alone: vestibular/ocular dysfunction, neck (cervicogenic) involvement, or an exercise-intolerance pattern — and can clear you, or set your restart pace, with real data instead of guesswork.
